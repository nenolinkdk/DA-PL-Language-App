# Course Structure — DA-PL

## Development rule

Create the entire course/module structure now, but fully populate **Niveau 1 first**.

## Niveau 1 — absolute beginner / practical Poland

Initial lesson plan:

1. Hej, goddag og høflighed
2. Ja, nej, tak og undskyld
3. Tal, priser og betaling
4. På café
5. Restaurant og mad
6. Butik og indkøb
7. Hotel og overnatning
8. Tog, bus og billetter
9. Vejvisning og steder
10. Hjælp og enkle problemer

Each lesson should eventually contain practical bilingual items, TTS and a small quiz.

Niveau 1 should optimize for recognition and usable phrases rather than grammatical completeness.

Phase 1C drafts all ten lessons in `content/course/level1`. The Polish and Danish text needs linguistic review before it is treated as final.

## Niveau 2 — everyday Polish + grammar in small steps

Structure exists from the beginning; production content follows Niveau 1.

Themes may include:
- everyday routines;
- meeting people;
- family;
- appointments;
- services;
- longer shopping situations;
- transport;
- simple work situations;
- time/date;
- practical digital communication.

Polish cases are introduced explicitly here, gradually and contextually. Phase 1C drafts ten lessons in `content/course/level2`. The examples introduce pronouns, present tense, gender, accusative, `do`/`na`/`w`, polite requests, and a few past and future phrases. This order is not frozen.

**LINGUISTIC REVIEW REQUIRED:** final order of cases and their scope, and the draft wording in both levels.

## Niveau 3 — extended/professional use

Reserved structure for:
- work and professional communication;
- longer conversations;
- practical written communication;
- more complex grammar;
- aspect and broader case control;
- problem solving.

**SCOPE NOT YET FROZEN.**

## Samtaletræning

Separate top-level module.

Initial scenarios overlap with Level 1 and Level 2 vocabulary:
- café;
- buying a ticket;
- asking the way;
- checking into a hotel;
- simple shop purchase;
- asking for repetition/help.

Those six scenarios are in `content/dialogues`. Danish support is hidden until the learner asks. FSM scenarios must remain short enough for phone use. There are no weighted transitions.

## Grammar

Reference module rather than a prerequisite wall.

Level 1 grammar pages should explain only immediately useful patterns.
Level 2 begins systematic but small-step case teaching.

## Children

Create structural support from the start. Full production content may be completed later.

## Content QA

Polish production content must be reviewed for:
- natural contemporary Polish;
- correct inflection;
- appropriate register;
- useful Danish equivalents rather than mechanically literal translations;
- TTS-suitable punctuation/text;
- beginner appropriateness;
- cultural/pragmatic correctness.
